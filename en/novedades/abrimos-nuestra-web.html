Our website is up (menu included)

21 September 2026

We kept having the same phone conversation: “what time do you open?”, “do you have the Teide dessert today?”, “where exactly are you?”. So we built a page that keeps it all in one place.

The menu, always current

The menu section comes from the same spreadsheet we use in the kitchen, so the dishes and prices you see here are the ones on the table.

Everything else, where you would expect it

Full opening hours, the address with a map, and the phone number one tap away. We open Wednesday to Sunday, lunch only, and booking is still as simple as calling +34 922 51 50 66.

We will use this news section for anything out of the ordinary: seasonal dishes, closing days, celebrations. See you at the table.
