Guachin Salsa this Saturday the 14th

9 November 2026

A date has been added to November, and it falls on a Saturday: there is Guachin Salsa at La Carreta on the 14th.

Why we are saying so now

The dates follow no fixed pattern: they are confirmed when they are confirmed and we announce them the same day. This one is less than a week away, which is only just enough time to book.

In case it is your first time

There is food, there is music and there is dancing. Anyone coming just for dinner has a place too: the table is set all the same.

The room is the size it is. When the places are gone, they are gone.

To book, call us or send a WhatsApp to 922 51 50 66. After the 14th come Friday the 20th and Friday the 27th: they are in the Guachin Salsa section and we announce them on Instagram too.
