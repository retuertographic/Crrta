Cookie policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files downloaded to the user's device when they visit a website. They make it possible to store and retrieve information about browsing habits or device use, and they can be used to recognise the user, personalise their experience and improve the services offered.

Types of cookies

By who manages them

  • First-party cookies: managed directly by the website owner and sent from its own domains.
  • Third-party cookies: managed by external entities that process the information obtained through the cookies.

By purpose

  • Technical cookies: needed for the website to work properly and to allow browsing and use of its services.
  • Personalisation cookies: remember certain user preferences, such as language or regional settings.
  • Analytics or measurement cookies: allow user behaviour to be analysed and usage statistics to be obtained in order to improve how the site works.
  • Behavioural advertising cookies: allow personalised advertising to be shown based on the user's browsing habits.

By retention period

  • Session cookies: deleted automatically when the browser is closed.
  • Persistent cookies: stored on the user's device for a set period.

Why we use cookies

Cookies are used to improve the user experience, personalise browsing, analyse use of the website, manage the session, ensure security and, where applicable, show personalised advertising. They also make it possible to obtain statistical information in order to optimise the contents and services offered.

Legal basis for the processing

The use of cookies is based on the user's consent, except for technical cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to work, which are based on the controller's legitimate interest.

Such transfers will be made only to countries with an adequate level of protection or under appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.
