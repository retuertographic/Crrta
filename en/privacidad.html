Principles applying to the processing

The personal data provided will be processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, and limited to what is strictly necessary for the purposes for which it is processed. The controller adopts the technical and organisational measures needed to guarantee the security, integrity and confidentiality of the data, preventing its alteration, loss, unauthorised processing or unauthorised access.

Personal data processed

Through this website and other communication channels, the controller may process, among others, the following data: identification data, postal and e-mail addresses, contact information, and commercial data relating to the services requested.

Specifically, the forms on this website collect the data you type into them:

  • Contact and booking form: name, e-mail address, telephone number and the message you write to us.
  • Catering quote form: name, e-mail address, telephone number, number of guests, date and place of the event, type of celebration, and a free-text field for allergies, timings and anything else we should know.

If you write to us by e-mail or call us, we will process the data you provide in that communication. If the user provides third-party data, they declare that they have previously informed those people of the content of this policy and obtained their consent where necessary.

Purposes of the processing

  • Responding to enquiries, requests for information or messages received.
  • Handling quotes, bookings, contracts and the provision of the services requested.
  • Maintaining the commercial and administrative relationship with customers and potential customers.
  • Sending commercial or informative communications, where there is consent or a sufficient legal basis.
  • Complying with the legal obligations applicable to the controller.

A booking requested through the form is not confirmed until you receive our reply.

Legal basis for the processing

  • The consent of the data subject, where required.
  • Performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures at the data subject's request.

  • The legitimate interest of the controller, where applicable and in line with current legislation.

Data retention

Personal data will be kept for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected and to address possible legal liabilities, unless the applicable legislation sets a different retention period.

Disclosure of data to third parties

Personal data will not be disclosed to third parties except where legally required. Where applicable, data may be disclosed to public authorities and tax, employment or judicial authorities. No international data transfers are planned.
