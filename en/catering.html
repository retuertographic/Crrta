Cuban food catering

Habana Expressthe taste of Cuba at your celebration

Bring the real taste of Cuba to your celebration with Habana Express. We cater private events, family gatherings, celebrations and corporate get-togethers.

Our team prepares and serves a selection of Cuban dishes as a buffet, sized to your guest count and to what each event needs. We provide the food, the equipment required and the staff who run the service.

What kind of event

  • Private events
  • Family gatherings
  • Celebrations
  • Corporate get-togethers

What we bring

  • The food, served as a buffet
  • The equipment the service needs
  • The staff looking after it during the event

Drinks are not included in the catering service.
