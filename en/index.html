Our story

Cuban rootsCanarian heart

From a small wooden house to a table where two cultures meet.

Our story starts with our Cuban roots and with one hope: to share our food and build a family project in Tenerife.

After training as a chef in Cuba, I came to the Canaries with my wife, who trained professionally as a pastry chef. Family ties brought us to this island, where we started looking for a place to make our dream of opening a restaurant real.

We finally found our place in El Carretón, in the municipality of Arafo. It all began in a small wooden house, with a modest porch and barely eight tables.

From the start our idea was clear: to bring the flavours of Cuba to Tenerife. We began working with Canarian pork, a deeply rooted tradition on the islands, while little by little we brought in the Cuban dishes we had always wanted to share. And so, quite naturally, our Cuban roots met the flavours and customs of the Canaries.

And where does our name come from?

In Cuba there was a restaurant called La Carreta that I had always liked a lot. When we found our place in El Carretón, it occurred to me to play with the two names. That is how La Carreta del Carretón was born — a way of joining that memory of Cuba to the corner of Tenerife where our new story was beginning.

A name that also had something very simple behind it: so people would know we were the Carreta that is in El Carretón.

Sixteen years sharing our table

Sixteen years have passed since those beginnings. The small wooden house has grown with us, and those eight tables are now the 27 we have today. But one thing has not changed: our eagerness to cook, to welcome people warmly and to make every visit a good occasion to share.

La Carreta today

Cuba and the Canaries at the same table

Today, La Carreta del Carretón is a family restaurant where the flavours of Cuban cooking and Canarian tradition live side by side. On our menu you will find long-standing Cuban recipes such as tamal, ropa vieja or congrí rice; dishes off the grill; and specialities of our own, like our gofio croquettes with rib meat, which capture that meeting of the two kitchens.

We also give particular care to the desserts, made with the dedication and affection that have always been part of our family project. We like home cooking, generous portions and that close atmosphere where a meal can turn into a long afternoon at the table.

Good food tastes even better when it is shared.

About allergens

Under each dish you will find the allergens it contains according to its usual recipe. Where it says «Gluten-free on request», the dish contains gluten as served, but we can make it without if you say so when ordering.

If a dish has no labels, it means we do not expect any of the fourteen allergens that must be declared.

Everything is cooked in the same kitchen, so we cannot guarantee there are no traces. If you have an allergy or an intolerance, tell us when you book or when you sit down and we will go through the menu with you.
