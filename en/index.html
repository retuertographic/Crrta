A Cuban kitchenin the heart of Arafo

La Carreta del Carretón is a small family restaurant in the old town of Arafo, where Cuban food is cooked the way it is cooked at home: slowly, with local produce and recipes from the island.

Checked tablecloths, a volcanic stone wall and a leafy patio. It fills up, so it is best to call ahead and book your table.

  • Home-style Cuban cooking
  • Pork is our speciality
  • Generous portions
  • Old-fashioned welcome
What everyone orders

Three reasonsto sit down with us

Off the grill

Ribs, pork and beef sautéed with peppers — the right amount of fire and a sauce that calls for bread.

The Teide dessert

Our own volcano: a mountain of sponge cake buried in chocolate. There is a reason it arrives with several spoons.

Table and conversation

Indoor dining room and a leafy patio, with tables for groups and families. A place to linger a little longer.
